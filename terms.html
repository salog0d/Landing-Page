Terms of Service

Last updated: October 21, 2026

These terms apply when you engage Fantazy Labz for a website project. They are a general reference: each project is also governed by its signed proposal and statement of work.

Services

We design and build websites for service businesses: strategy, messaging, UX and UI, development, search foundations and analytics. The exact deliverables for your project are described in your proposal and statement of work.

Proposals and scope

Every engagement starts with a written proposal that defines deliverables, number of pages and templates, revision rounds, dependencies, exclusions and acceptance criteria. Changes outside the agreed scope are handled through written change requests before work begins.

Payments

We invoice in USD. Payment schedules and milestones are defined per project — for example, 40% to start, 30% at an agreed milestone and 30% before launch. Your schedule is written into your proposal.

Your responsibilities

  • Appointing one decision-maker for scope, content and approvals
  • Providing content, photos, video and access in the agreed formats and timeframes
  • Reviewing deliverables and giving feedback within the agreed windows

Ownership and intellectual property

Once your project is fully paid, you receive the agreed source, accounts, credentials and handoff materials, and you own the deliverables created for your project. We retain ownership of our pre-existing tools, templates and intellectual property.

No guaranteed outcomes

We build websites around a clear conversion hypothesis, but we cannot guarantee specific levels of traffic, rankings, leads or revenue. Results depend on factors outside our control, including your market, content and offer.

Warranties and liability

We deliver work to the quality standards agreed in your statement of work. To the maximum extent permitted by law, our liability for any claim related to a project is limited to the fees you paid for that project.

Third-party services

Projects may rely on third-party services such as hosting, CMS platforms, form processors or analytics. Those services are governed by their own terms, and we set up accounts in your name where practical.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Nuevo León, Mexico. Any dispute will first be addressed in good faith between the parties.
