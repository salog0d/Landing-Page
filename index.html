FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does a website project cost?

Most projects start between 2,500 and 7,500 USD depending on scope. The final investment is defined by page templates, content, integrations, migration and functionality — written into the proposal before you commit, so there are no surprises.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope, content and feedback speed. We agree on checkpoints and a realistic timeline in the proposal, and you see progress along the way instead of waiting until the end.

Where is your team based?

Fantazy Labz is a Mexico based remote technology studio. Our workflow supports US time zone collaboration, clear milestones and direct communication throughout the project.

Who provides the content?

We structure the messaging, hierarchy and copy requirements for you. Final copy, photos and video are usually provided or coordinated with your team — we define responsibilities before the project starts.

Do I own the website?

Yes. On completion you receive the agreed source, accounts, credentials and handoff materials. Domains and accounts are set up in your name where practical.

What happens after launch?

You can run the site on your own or choose a care plan — Website Care or Growth Care — for monitoring, updates, optimization and ongoing improvements.

What do you need from me to start?

A decision-maker, your current website (if you have one), any existing assets, and a clear idea of what the new site should accomplish. We handle the rest, starting with discovery.

How do you work with clients remotely?

We publish collaboration hours with US time zone overlap, agree on checkpoints in writing, and keep communication direct with the team building your site. You review progress at every milestone.
