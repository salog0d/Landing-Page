Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how Fantazy Labz collects, uses and protects the information you share with us through this website. We keep it short and in plain language.

Who we are

Fantazy Labz is a technology studio based in Monterrey, Mexico, that designs and builds websites for service businesses. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at proyectos@fantazylabz.tech.

Information we collect

When you use our forms, we collect only what you choose to share, such as:

  • Your name, company and work email
  • Your website URL (for website reviews)
  • Project details, budget range, timeline and goals you provide

We do not collect payment information on this website, and we do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiries and deliver your website review
  • To evaluate whether your project is a good fit
  • To prepare proposals and communicate about your project
  • To improve our services and understand aggregate demand

Legal basis

We process the information you submit on the basis of your consent (when you send a form) and our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries.

Processing and storage

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, our form provider, and stored with that processor. We keep inquiries only as long as they are relevant to the conversation, and we never sell or rent your information to third parties.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate site usage — for example, which pages are visited and which forms are submitted. This data is used to improve the site, not to identify individual visitors.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete it. Email proyectos@fantazylabz.tech and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

This website uses minimal, functional cookies only where necessary. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Third parties

We rely on two service providers: Formspree (form processing) and Netlify (hosting). Each processes data under its own security and privacy terms.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and note the date above.
